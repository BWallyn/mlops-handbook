## Your assignment

Now that you have learned how to find the best hyperparameters for a model doing Bayesian optimization using **Optuna**, it's time for you to apply it to your project. The goal is for you to automate the research of the best hyperparameters before training your final model.


### Finding the best hyperparameters

To get the best performance of your model, optimizing the hyperparameters is a good point. To do so, you can use the bayesian optimization using the optuna package.

What should you do?
1. Look for the hyperparameters of your model you want to optimize. A good starting point would be to look at the documentation fo your model.
2. Define a function to run the bayesian optimization. Use the train set to train each intermediate model and the validation set to compute the performance of the combination of the hyperparameters.
3. Get the best combination of the hyperparameters and use them to train your final model on train+validation and analyze on the test set.
